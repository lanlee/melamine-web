Real Use Cases

Where Melamine Powers the World

From kitchen surfaces to construction sites — melamine is everywhere.

Decorative Laminates & Furniture

Melamine-impregnated paper laminated onto MDF creates the scratch-resistant, hygienic surfaces used in furniture, cabinets, and flooring worldwide.

~40% of global melamine demand

Dishware & Kitchenware

Melamine-formaldehyde molding compounds are used to produce lightweight, impact-resistant tableware — bowls, plates, trays — favored in foodservice and hospitality.

500M+ units produced annually

Construction & Concrete

Melamine-based superplasticizers dramatically reduce water content in concrete mixes, improving strength and workability for high-performance structural applications.

Used in 60%+ of major infrastructure projects

Flame Retardants

Melamine and its derivatives (cyanurate, polyphosphate) are non-halogen flame retardants used in cables, textiles, foam, and electronic components to meet global fire safety standards.

REACH & RoHS compliant grades available

Paper & Textile Treatment

Melamine-urea resins are used for wet-strength treatment of paper products and crease-resistance finishing in cotton and blended textiles.

Key input for premium tissue manufacturers

Coatings & Adhesives

MF resins serve as cross-linking agents in alkyd, acrylic, and polyester coatings, providing hardness, gloss, and chemical resistance to industrial and automotive finishes.

Used by top automotive OEMs globally
