## What separates a manufacturer from a reseller

Most pages ranking for this term sell melamine board, sponges or tableware — not powder. The buyer's question is narrower: who converts urea into melamine, and who merely repackages it? [Laminates and wood adhesives account for about 70% of global melamine demand](https://cornerstonechemco.com/melamine), so a supplier who cannot describe your process is a warning sign.

## The 7 checks that decide a melamine order

1. **Name the plant, not the brand.** Ask which reactor the powder comes from and under which licensed technology. [Eurotecnica's Euromel process alone has 24 licensees running 33 plants, with over 1.5 million tonnes a year of licensed capacity](https://eurotecnica.it/technologies/euromel-melamine/). A supplier that will not name a plant cannot support a claim.

2. **Match the grade to your process.** Laminate and adhesive makers need high-purity powder; coatings and moulding compounds work to different specs. [OCI Global, which calls itself the world's leading melamine producer, ships ISCC PLUS-certified bio-melamine made from biomethane, reporting a greenhouse-gas reduction above 40%](https://oci-global.com/news-stories/stories/biomelamine-decarbonization-partnership-foresa/) — ask about it if your customers audit emissions.

3. **Read the batch documents first.** Every shipment should arrive with a COA and MSDS. Top-grade powder runs ≥99.8% purity with ≤0.1% moisture and ≤0.03% ash; check the COA against the spec you ordered, not the sales email.

4. **Check duty exposure on your route.** [US anti-dumping and countervailing duty orders on melamine from China entered their second five-year sunset review on 1 June 2026](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/01/2026-10910/melamine-from-china-institution-of-five-year-reviews), and separate orders cover [Germany, Japan, the Netherlands and Trinidad and Tobago](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/31/2025-01999/melamine-from-germany-japan-the-netherlands-and-trinidad-and-tobago-antidumping-duty-orders). Landed cost, not FOB price, decides.

5. **Benchmark the quote against an index.** [China's March 2026 melamine exports averaged US$744.10 per tonne across 72,800 tonnes, and China accounts for more than 60% of global melamine trade](https://www.sunsirs.com/commodity-news/petail-32433.html). When a quote sits far below the index, ask what changed — grade, packaging or testing.

6. **Test the minimum order.** Container-scale producers and 1 MT suppliers serve different buyers. Order small once, assay it, then commit.

7. **Confirm packaging and paperwork.** Melamine ships under HS code 2933.61 in 25 kg bags or bulk; agree who prepares export documents and who bears a failed assay.

## Key Takeaways

- A melamine manufacturer should name its plant, technology and reactor.
- Batch documents — COA, MSDS and a test report — outweigh a headline purity figure.
- Duty status changes landed cost — the US orders on Chinese melamine entered a second sunset review on 1 June 2026.
- Benchmark quotes against published export averages, not only competing bids.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What purity should industrial melamine powder be?

Top-grade industrial melamine is typically ≥99.8% purity with ≤0.1% moisture; first-grade material is ≥99.0%. Compare the batch COA against the specification you ordered.

### How much does melamine cost per tonne in 2026?

China's average melamine export price was US$744.10 per tonne in March 2026, per customs data reported by SunSirs. Grade, packaging, volume and Incoterms move the figure.

### Do US buyers pay anti-dumping duty on Chinese melamine?

Possibly. AD/CVD orders on melamine from China date to 2015 and entered a second sunset review on 1 June 2026. Confirm the rate with your customs broker before fixing landed cost.
