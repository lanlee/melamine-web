## TL;DR

Melamine is a nitrogen-rich organic compound (C3H6N6, CAS 108-78-1) made from urea — 66% nitrogen by mass. Reacted with formaldehyde it becomes the resin behind laminates, dinnerware, adhesives and flame retardants. Buy on the Certificate of Analysis, not the brochure: 99.8% for laminates, 99.5% for additives.

## Key Takeaways

- Melamine's value appears once condensed into MF resin.
- Purity splits into 3 grades: 99.8% premium, 99.5% industrial, 99.0% technical.
- FDA-approved for tableware and coatings — never for direct addition to food or feed.

## What Is Melamine?

Melamine is a white crystalline solid, CAS 108-78-1, made from urea — 6 (NH2)2CO → C3H6N6 + 6 NH3 + 3 CO2 — with the ammonia recycled into the urea loop. Melamine prices therefore track urea and energy more than melamine supply. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melamine)

That 66% nitrogen content matters twice: it drives resin performance, and it defeats standard protein assays — the mechanism behind the 2008 dairy scandal that affected some 300,000 infants. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melamine)

## The 6 Industrial Uses of Melamine

Melamine is almost never used alone; condensed with formaldehyde into MF resin, it covers all industrial demand.

1. **Decorative laminates** — the scratch-resistant skin on worktops, flooring and cabinets.
2. **Dinnerware** — moulded MF resin gives shatter-resistant, dishwasher-safe plates.
3. **Coatings and adhesives** — crosslinkers that harden enamels and bind plywood and MDF.
4. **Concrete superplasticizers** — cut water content while keeping workability.
5. **Flame retardants** — release nitrogen to dilute combustible gases.
6. **Paper and textiles** — wet-strength for paper, crease resistance for fabric.

## The 3 Melamine Purity Grades

| Grade | Minimum purity | Typical applications |
|---|---|---|
| Premium | 99.8% | Laminates, light-coloured goods, food-contact dinnerware, clear coatings |
| Industrial | 99.5% | Concrete additives, flame retardants, adhesives, paper and textile |
| Technical | 99.0% | Non-critical internal processes; rare in trade |

Dropping a grade raises the odds of off-white product. The COA proves the grade; a brochure only states an intention — see [the grades we stock](https://www.hanchengmaterial.com/#products).

## Is Melamine Safe?

For its intended uses, yes. The FDA allows melamine in manufacturing cooking utensils, plates, paper, paperboard and industrial coatings — but not direct addition to human food or animal feed. [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/economically-motivated-adulteration-food-fraud/melamine-tableware-questions-and-answers)

| Benchmark | Value |
|---|---|
| FDA acceptable level, foods other than infant formula | 2,500 ppb |
| Estimated migration into cold orange juice, ~15 minutes | Under 10 ppb |

In FDA's own tests only 3 of 19 plates released measurable melamine, and only when food was held at 160 °F for two hours — about 250 times below the agency's limit. [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/economically-motivated-adulteration-food-fraud/melamine-tableware-questions-and-answers)

In 2019 the IARC classified melamine as possibly carcinogenic (Group 2B): inadequate evidence in humans, sufficient in animals. [IARC](https://www.env-health.org/why-melamine-should-be-added-to-reachs-blacklist-of-harmful-chemicals/)

**The rule:** never microwave food on melamine dinnerware. Heat it in ceramic, then serve.

## 5 Checks Before You Buy Melamine Powder

1. Write minimum purity into the purchase order — 99.8% for laminates, 99.5% for additives.
2. Ask for COA, MSDS and SGS reports before negotiating price, not after.
3. Match the COA to the lot number on your packing list.
4. Fix bag size, pallets and Incoterms first; they move landed cost more than unit price.
5. Run incoming QC on appearance, moisture and assay before final payment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why can't you microwave melamine?

Heat drives residual melamine out of the resin into food, most strongly with acidic food. The FDA says plainly that food and drink should not be heated on melamine dinnerware in a microwave oven.

### What purity of melamine should I buy?

Use 99.8% minimum for laminates, light-coloured goods and dinnerware; about 99.5% for additives, flame retardants and adhesives.

### Is melamine safe to eat off?

Yes. FDA testing found migration below 10 ppb into cold acidic food, about 250 times below its own 2,500 ppb limit. Do not microwave it.
