## What counts as a melamine product?

"Melamine products" covers two groups. Retail shoppers mean finished goods: plates, bowls, serving trays, countertops, and cabinet panels. Industrial buyers mean the materials those goods start from, plus the adhesives, coatings, textile finishes, and flame retardants built on the same resin. Both trace back to one raw material — melamine, a white crystalline compound with the formula [C3N3(NH2)3](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/Melamine).

## How melamine products are made, step by step

1. **Start with the powder.** The molder weighs melamine molding powder precisely, because purity decides the finished piece's strength, color stability, and food-contact performance. This is why we ship every HanCheng batch with COA, MSDS, and SGS test reports — the molder knows the 99.8% purity before pressing, not after a failed run. (See [what to check before ordering melamine powder](https://www.hanchengmaterial.com/blog/melamine-powder).)
2. **React it into resin.** Melamine bonds with formaldehyde to form a thermosetting resin that permanently hardens once cured — the chemistry is covered in [our melamine formaldehyde resin guide](https://www.hanchengmaterial.com/blog/melamine-formaldehyde-resin).
3. **Shape it under heat and pressure.** Dinnerware is compression-molded in heated steel molds at roughly 150–180°C, cured in seconds, then trimmed and polished. Decorative panels take a different route: resin-soaked paper is thermally fused onto particleboard or MDF — what panel makers call thermally fused laminate or low-pressure laminate [according to distributor AWI](https://www.awi-wa.com/products/decorative-surfaces/melamine/).
4. **Finish and inspect.** Edges are ground, surfaces polished, decorative decals fused and re-cured, and each piece is checked for weight, shape, and color before packing.

## The main product families at a glance

| Product family | How it is made | Where you see it |
|---|---|---|
| Molded dinnerware | Melamine-formaldehyde molding powder compression-molded in heated steel molds | Plates, bowls, kids' tableware, serving trays |
| Decorative panels (TFL) | Resin-impregnated paper thermally fused to particleboard or MDF | Cabinets, shelving, wardrobe interiors, store fixtures |
| High-pressure laminates | Stacked resin-soaked papers pressed at higher pressure | Countertops, tabletops, work surfaces |
| Industrial materials | Resin used as a base or additive | Adhesives, coatings, textile and paper finishes, flame retardants, concrete plasticizers |

## What the safety data actually shows

Two peer-reviewed studies matter to buyers. A 2024 study of 46 bowls and cups sold in China confirmed that "bamboo" and "wheat straw" tableware is melamine-formaldehyde resin with plant fiber added, and measured total migration highest in bamboo-made pieces and lowest in glass and ceramic [Liu et al., Toxics, 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38393238/). If you market eco-friendly dinnerware, verify what the substrate really is. A 2017 Italian study of 90 samples measured melamine and formaldehyde migration in new, used, and artificially aged tableware; migration rose as the resin degraded, and the melamine migration limit was exceeded after ageing [Mannoni et al., 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27824529/). The practical rule: retire scratched or worn pieces. Neither study supports alarm — both support honest care instructions, which [resin-grade quality control](https://www.hanchengmaterial.com/blog/melamine-resin) protects.

## Key Takeaways

- Every melamine product starts as melamine powder reacted with formaldehyde into a thermosetting resin.
- Molding makes dinnerware; thermal fusing makes panels and laminates — same resin, different route.
- Bamboo and wheat straw tableware are melamine-formaldehyde resin with plant fiber, per 2024 research.
- Migration rises with heat, acidity, and wear.
- Powder purity sets the ceiling for finished-product quality.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are melamine products made of?

Melamine-formaldehyde resin: melamine powder bonded with formaldehyde and cured under heat and pressure into a hard, stable thermoset.

### Can melamine products go in the microwave?

No — manufacturers advise against microwave heating because migration rises with temperature and acidity. Use ceramic or glass when heating food.

### Are bamboo and wheat straw plates melamine?

Yes. A 2024 study found they are melamine-formaldehyde resin with bamboo or wheat straw fiber added, not fiber-only alternatives.
